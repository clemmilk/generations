# Générations : traduction anglaise

Traductions utilisées dans le livret anglais (`docs/rulebook_generations_en.pdf`), version du 18/10.
Les effets sont écrits à l'impératif, comme dans le livret (« Gain 3 Gold. »). Pour garder la tournure à la troisième personne du csv (« Gagne 3 Or »), il suffit d'ajouter un « s » au verbe : « Gains 3 Gold ».

## Les 20 Privilèges

Texte des tuiles, à la troisième personne comme dans le csv. La version du livret, à la deuxième personne, figure en dessous.

| N° | Texte de la tuile (comme le csv) | Texte du livret |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | All cards in the common deck cost 2 Gold less. | River cards cost you 2 Gold less (never below 0; the Resource cost does not change). |
| 2 | Each Advanced card also provides 1 Scholar. | Each Advanced card in your play area also provides 1 Scholar. |
| 3 | Each Advanced card also provides 1 Stone. | Each Advanced card in your play area also provides 1 Stone. |
| 4 | Each Advanced card also provides 1 Metal. | Each Advanced card in your play area also provides 1 Metal. |
| 5 | 1 extra Population crest. | 1 extra Population crest. |
| 6 | 1 extra Economy crest. | 1 extra Economy crest. |
| 7 | 1 extra Nobility crest. | 1 extra Nobility crest. |
| 8 | At the start of each new lineage, the player draws 1 card fewer than usual, then takes 1 card of their choice from their deck, before shuffling it. | Start of a lineage: draw 1 card fewer than usual, then take 1 card of your choice from your deck before shuffling it (mandatory). |
| 9 | At the start of each new lineage, the player gains 2 extra Gold for each Population crest they have. | Start of a lineage: gain 2 extra Gold per Population crest. |
| 10 | At the start of each new lineage, the player gains 2 extra Gold for each Nobility crest they have. | Start of a lineage: gain 2 extra Gold per Nobility crest. |
| 11 | At the start of each new lineage, the player gains 2 extra Gold for each Economy crest they have. | Start of a lineage: gain 2 extra Gold per Economy crest. |
| 12 | At the start of each new lineage, the player draws 1 extra card, then discards 1 card from their hand (discarded, not destroyed). | Start of a lineage: draw 1 extra card, then discard 1 card from your hand (mandatory; it is not destroyed). |
| 13 | On their turn, instead of their action, the player may pay 3 Stone to destroy 1 card (once per turn). | Instead of your action: pay 3 Stone to destroy 1 card. |
| 14 | On their turn, instead of their action, the player may pay 2 Metal to draw 1 card (once per turn). | Instead of your action: pay 2 Metal to draw 1 card. |
| 15 | On their turn, instead of their action, the player may pay 6 Gold to draw 1 card (once per turn). | Instead of your action: pay 6 Gold to draw 1 card. |
| 16 | 1 Scholar symbol, valid for every effect requiring Scholars, as if it were on a card in their play area. | 1 permanent Scholar, as if it were on a card in your play area, including to gain a Privilege. |
| 17 | Each time the player destroys 1 card, they gain 2 Gold. | Each time you destroy 1 card, including the card replaced by a purchase, gain 2 Gold. |
| 18 | Each time the player ends their lineage, after raising their Economy track, they keep their remaining Gold. | When you end a lineage, keep your remaining Gold instead of discarding it. |
| 19 | At the start of each new lineage, the player gains 3 extra Gold. | Start of a lineage: gain 3 extra Gold. |
| 20 | Each time the player plays 1 Advanced card, they gain 2 Gold. | Each time you play an Advanced card, gain 2 Gold, even if you do not apply its effect. |
